READ THE RESULTS WITH CARE

What this analysis can tell you.

It describes one anonymized workbook. It cannot certify a current website, establish real-world error rates, or reproduce the original feature extraction.

  • No collection-date fields are present, so current threat coverage and change over time cannot be assessed.
  • Anonymized URLs and domains cannot validate real brands, exact addresses, reputation, or live behavior.
  • No training, tuning, or held-out evaluation was performed. The reported shares are full-dataset counts.
  • Only aggregate statistics and column names are included here. Raw dataset records are not bundled with the extension.
