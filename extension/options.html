    PRIVACY

    Your page stays on your device.

    When it runs
    With automatic checks enabled, when an HTTP or HTTPS webpage loads. Select “Scan again” to run a manual check or refresh one after page content changes. Chrome restricts checks on browser settings and some protected pages.
    What it checks
    The current web address and selected page structure, such as form destinations, links, and the presence of password fields.
    What it never reads
    Password text, form field values, cookies, or saved browser history. It does not submit forms or change the website.
    What it stores
    Domains you mark as familiar stay in local extension storage until you remove them or uninstall. Recent scan results are temporary.
    What leaves your device
    Nothing from a scan. There is no analytics, advertising, account, or external reputation service.

    SYNTHID

    Not checked by TideCheck.

    TideCheck does not detect SynthID watermarks. Use Google's detector to check selected media. No media is uploaded by TideCheck. A missing detection does not mean content is human-made.

    LIMITS

    A useful pause, not a guarantee.

    TideCheck uses explainable checks. Its concern levels are not trained-model probabilities. Legitimate websites can trigger warnings, and phishing pages can avoid them.

    Checks cover the current page and the information the browser makes available. They cannot verify the organization behind a website, inspect every embedded frame, or predict what a link will do after you follow it.

    If a request for a password, payment, or personal information feels unexpected, navigate to the organization through an address you already trust or contact it directly.
