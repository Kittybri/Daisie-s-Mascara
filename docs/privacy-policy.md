# Daisie Mascara privacy policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Publication status: draft. The publisher identity, contact address, and public policy URL must be completed before Chrome Web Store submission.

## What Daisie Mascara does

Daisie Mascara checks supported HTTP and HTTPS webpages automatically after loading or tab activation. Automatic checks can be disabled in settings; manual checks remain available. Its findings explain rule-based clues. They do not certify a website or estimate a validated probability of phishing.

## Information processed on your device

When an automatic or manual scan runs, Daisie Mascara processes the page address and limited structural information to produce the result. It does not read the values entered in password or other form fields. It does not send page addresses, page content, or scan results to a server.

Daisie Mascara does not maintain a saved browsing-history log. The current scan result may remain temporarily in extension memory while the extension is running.

If you mark a domain as familiar, Daisie Mascara stores that domain in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. This preference helps the extension show your own choice the next time you inspect that domain. It is not a statement that the domain is legitimate.

## Data sharing and analytics

Daisie Mascara has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or external scanning service. The publisher does not receive or sell your browsing information. No data is transferred to third parties by the extension.

## Your controls

You can turn automatic checks off, request a manual scan, remove individual familiar domains, or clear all familiar-domain choices in settings. The automatic-check preference is saved locally. Removing the extension through Chrome's extension manager stops its checks and removes its extension storage.

The dataset view contains bundled aggregate counts and the schema of a supplied anonymized research workbook. It does not contain the raw website records. The SynthID link opens Google's external detector. Daisie Mascara does not upload media or read that service's results; any media you choose to upload there is handled by Google under its policies.

## Permissions

- **scripting:** runs the included local scanner in that page.
- **storage:** saves and removes familiar-domain and automatic-check preferences locally.
- **HTTP/HTTPS website access:** lets the extension check supported pages after they load without requiring a click on every page.

The extension has access to HTTP and HTTPS webpages for its automatic checks. Some browser pages cannot be scanned because Chrome restricts extension access to them.
